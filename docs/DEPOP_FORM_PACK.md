# Depop Form Pack

FlipLab now mirrors the major fields shown by Depop's web listing form so the AI report covers more than description and price.

## Covered fields

- Photos guidance (up to 8 on Depop web)
- Description + hashtags
- Category
- Brand
- Condition
- Up to 2 colors
- Size
- Quantity
- Auto-generated SKU
- Source
- Age / era
- Up to 3 style tags
- Material
- Three pricing targets: likely to sell, balanced, less likely
- Offer floor
- Shipping method
- Package-size suggestion
- Shipping-address reminder
- Measurements to add

## Truthfulness rules

FlipLab does not invent unsupported facts. Source and age/era remain `Not specified` unless the listing text or AI output gives evidence. Package size is a suggestion and should be verified against the packaged item. Account-level fields such as the seller shipping address remain tied to the user's Depop account.

The three pricing targets are FlipLab suggestions modeled after Depop's three-way pricing presentation; they are not represented as Depop's live market estimate.
